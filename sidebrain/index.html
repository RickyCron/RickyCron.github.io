Sidebrain

Your day as it really is.

Sidebrain is a Mac sidebar for university students. A thin glass rail on the edge of your screen opens into one panel for your day: what's on now, what's due soon, your lectures turned into notes and flashcards, your notes, meeting and lecture transcripts, and the messages that actually need you.

Status: private beta for a small group of students at the University of Nottingham. Not yet available to download publicly.

What it connects to

  • Your Google account, to sign in, show your calendar and flag priority emails.
  • Your university's Moodle, for course files and deadlines.
  • A folder of notes on your Mac.

Sidebrain never sends a message or email for you: replies only go when you press Send.
