Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. This policy covers the Sidebrain Mac app (beta), made by Ricky Cronson, Nottingham, UK. Contact: rickyjcronson@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Most of your data stays on your Mac.
  • Your app data is backed up to your own Sidebrain account so it survives a new laptop. Only you can read it.
  • Sidebrain never sells your data, never shows ads and never sends anything on your behalf without you pressing Send.

Google account data

If you connect Google, Sidebrain asks for:

  • Your name and email, to sign you in to your Sidebrain account.
  • Gmail (read, and send when you press Send): to find recent emails that need a reply or have a deadline, and to send the replies you write. Email content is processed to decide what is a priority. The decision and a short summary are stored; full email bodies are not stored on our servers.
  • Google Calendar: to show your schedule, and to add deadlines and study blocks to a separate "Sidebrain" calendar, which you can undo or delete.

Sidebrain's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features above, is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not used to train general AI models, and is not read by people except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires it.

AI processing

Some features use AI: priority screening, summaries, lecture notes and flashcards, and answering questions about your notes.

  • Your own AI: if you choose to use your own Claude or ChatGPT, that service processes the data under its own terms.
  • Built-in AI: the relevant text is sent to an AI provider (currently OpenAI) to produce the result. It is sent with data retention for training switched off where the provider allows it.
  • On your Mac: some tasks run entirely on-device using Apple's built-in models.

University data

If you connect your university's Moodle, Sidebrain stores a copy of your course files, deadlines and (if you press Sync) lecture transcripts on your Mac, using your own login.

Recordings and your voice

Meeting and lecture recordings, their transcripts and the optional sample of your voice used to label your lines as "You" stay on your Mac. They are never uploaded unless you choose an option that says it uploads audio. Only record where you have permission to.

Where data is stored

Your account and app data (tasks, deadlines, flashcards and similar) are stored with Supabase in the EU (Ireland). Security tokens are kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google, Moodle or any account in Settings at any time.
  • You can also remove Sidebrain's access to Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete your account and all stored data, email rickyjcronson@gmail.com. It will be deleted within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change, and important changes will be shown in the app.
